Statement

From the current series, We live our predictions

The ego, insecure and afraid for itself, tries to foresee and prepare for what it can find in what it remembers; everything else is discarded as unreal and moves into the future moment — into prediction, prognosis, probability. We try to alter the prediction, to raise or lower the probability. We wait for, and foresee, a future projection.

The series of paintings I have begun treats the human being as a screen for projection. The shape of the body, its posture, distort what is projected, as an attempt to influence the probability of what was predicted. The image projected onto the body represents the ego: how we think, or wish, others see us.

I believe a work of art should stand in contrast to what we project as reality. The presence of the unexpected, the improbable, should remind us of what we ignore and what we reject.

About

Nikola Milanović, born in 1977, is a painter based in Belgrade who works under the name nikoJart. He grew up surrounded by art and psychology — his father a sculptor, his mother a psychologist — and from a young age has questioned our perception of reality.

He studied at the Faculty of Fine Arts in Belgrade from 2000 to 2005: first in the class of Marija Dragojlović, then in the class of Professor Zoran Vuković and assistant Zoran Dimovski. His work has been shown in group exhibitions, from rented flats, taverns and cafés to galleries. From 2014 to 2019 he ran the NikoJart school of drawing and painting.

He works in oil on canvas and in chalk and acrylic on paper, often at large scale. The human figure is his constant subject.
